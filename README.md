## 🌍 THE PROBLEM

Humanity faces four interconnected crises that no single technology can solve:

| Crisis | Reality |
|:---|:---|
| 💧 **Water** | Agriculture consumes 70% of global freshwater. 40% of the world will face severe water stress by 2030. |
| ⚡ **Energy** | Intermittent renewables cannot provide 24/7 baseload. The world still burns fossil fuels to keep the lights on. |
| 🌾 **Food** | 9.8 billion people by 2050. Food production must increase 70%. Current systems cannot scale. |
| 🔋 **Materials** | Lithium supply chains depend on destructive open-pit mining. The energy transition needs critical minerals. |

**These are treated as separate problems by separate industries.**
**That is why they remain unsolved.**
