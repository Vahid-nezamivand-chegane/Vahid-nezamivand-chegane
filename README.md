## 🔬 About Me

**Independent researcher and systems architect** working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, medicine, water security, and climate resilience. Founder of **AquaNexus** — an integrated zero-carbon geothermal platform producing water, energy, food, and critical materials from a single investment.

- 🤖 **Artificial Intelligence** — Medical AI, surgical decision support, and domain-adaptive frameworks
- 💧 **Water-Energy-Food Systems** — Integrated infrastructure for water-stressed regions
- 🌍 **Climate Resilience** — Circular economy, carbon sequestration, and sustainable development
- 🔋 **Critical Materials** — Direct lithium extraction and strategic mineral recovery
